## About Me

I am an **Applied AI Researcher and Project Lead** working at the intersection of **responsible AI, AI for social good, machine learning, AI safety, sustainability, human-centered AI, and public-interest technology**.

My work focuses on building and evaluating AI systems that are not only technically capable, but also **reliable, transparent, socially responsible, and useful in real-world settings**.

A major motivation behind my work is **AI for social good**: exploring how artificial intelligence can support sustainability, public-interest challenges, nonprofit and public-sector initiatives, community-centered technologies, and applications that create meaningful social impact.

I hold a **PhD in Interdisciplinary Engineering from IIT Guwahati** and have worked across research, product, and project leadership roles involving globally distributed teams, applied AI prototypes, benchmarking workflows, stakeholder coordination, and interdisciplinary research translation.

I currently contribute to **Women in AI / WAI USA Research Labs** as a **Project Manager**, supporting collaborative research and applied AI initiatives with an emphasis on responsible, inclusive, and socially useful technology.

---

## Current Research Focus

- **LLM Evaluation & AI Robustness**  
  Evaluating LLM judges, benchmark reliability, post-decision manipulability, evaluator robustness, and trustworthy evaluation workflows.

- **Agentic AI Safety & Governance**  
  Risk-scoping tool-using AI systems, external action capability, autonomy, memory, approval mechanisms, auditability, and deployment safeguards.

- **Responsible AI & Trustworthy Deployment**  
  Developing practical approaches for evaluating reliability, transparency, evidence preservation, accountability, and responsible AI deployment.

- **AI for Social Good & Public Interest**  
  Applying AI to socially meaningful challenges across nonprofit, public-sector, community, sustainability, education, and development contexts.

- **AI for Sustainability & Climate**  
  Sustainability benchmarking, environmental analytics, climate-related applications, solar-energy systems, SDG-aligned technologies, and data-driven environmental decision support.

- **Human-Centered AI**  
  Ethical conversational AI, mental-health AI safety, human-robot interaction, interpretability, and community-centered technology.

- **Research-to-Practice Execution**  
  Translating research questions into usable datasets, evaluation pipelines, prototypes, reports, publications, and deployable systems.
